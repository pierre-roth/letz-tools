# LëtzTools terms of use

LëtzTools is an independent Luxembourgish writing aid maintained by Pierre Roth. These terms cover your use of the skill. The assistant platform and the external sources it uses have their own terms.

- Use LëtzTools for lawful language work and only with text you are authorized to process. Redact private information before using live checks. Do not use the skill to process sensitive personal data, abuse the language services or circumvent their access controls.
- The skill offers writing suggestions and references. Review the result before relying on it or sending it. A spelling check can miss errors or flag a valid form, and live reference services may be unavailable.
- LëtzTools is not an official product of the Zenter fir d’Lëtzebuerger Sprooch, OpenAI or Anthropic. Reference material and third-party components remain subject to their own rights and applicable terms.

See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for how live checks handle data. For help or to report a problem, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/pierre-roth/letz-tools/issues) and include only a non-sensitive example.
